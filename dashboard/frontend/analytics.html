InterpretationHow to read the analytics
Higher NDVI

Generally indicates stronger green vegetation presence. Compare areas over time rather than treating one value as a standalone ecological conclusion.

Stable

Vegetation conditions show a relatively balanced temporal pattern, with neither improving nor declining behaviour dominating the available weekly NDVI observations.

Declining

The available weekly NDVI observations indicate a predominantly negative vegetation trend. The area should be reviewed alongside field observations before restoration intervention decisions are made.
